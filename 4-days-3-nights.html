4 Days 3 Nights Rinjani Trekking

Experience the beauty of Mount Rinjani with Asgard Rinjani Trekking

About This Trek

Experience an exciting 4 Days 3 Nights trekking adventure on Mount Rinjani. Enjoy beautiful mountain landscapes, fresh air, and unforgettable moments in the heart of Lombok.

Duration
4 Days 3 Nights
Location
Mount Rinjani, Lombok
Difficulty
Moderate to Challenging

Itinerary

Day 1

Start your Rinjani trekking adventure from the chosen entrance gate. Trek through beautiful forest and mountain trails before reaching the first camping area.

Day 2

Continue the journey through the spectacular mountain landscape. Enjoy the views along the trail and spend another night surrounded by the beauty of Mount Rinjani.

Day 3

Experience the beauty of Mount Rinjani and continue exploring the mountain trails. Take time to enjoy the scenery before reaching the next camping area.

Day 4

Enjoy the final morning on Mount Rinjani, then continue the descent toward the finishing point. End your 4 Days 3 Nights adventure with unforgettable memories of Rinjani and Lombok.
